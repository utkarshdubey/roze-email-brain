# Four tiers of mail

Replied-or-starred mail in full for all time; two years of other inbox mail judged per sender by a small
model with deterministic caps; every other message as a header row plus its raw body stored as evidence;
`read_email` for the rest, live. Reading everything with a model costs dollars and adds noise; reading only
conversations misses offers, notices, and receipts. The reviewer confirmed this scope is acceptable.
