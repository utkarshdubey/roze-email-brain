# Open loops have a lifecycle

A loop needs material stakes (something asked of the user, or a relationship the user participates in);
a resolved source thread closes it; it leaves the current index after 365 days of silence or once every
named date has passed, and its history stays in the entity file. Cross-thread loop resolution is
deliberately absent: a plausible continuation is not proof a commitment closed.
