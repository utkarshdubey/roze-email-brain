# Topics are the third cluster family

Entity clusters join mail about one counterparty, while domain clusters join a broad part of life. An effort
can share neither and still recur. A third deterministic family groups normalized tag topics, augments them
with subject tokens, and merges token sets at Jaccard ≥ 0.5. Only function words are discarded; no person,
product, vendor, or mailbox vocabulary is encoded. Topic clusters use the entity minimum and cap, split by
year when oversized, and judge in their own eight-bucket cache namespace. The model prompt is unchanged.
